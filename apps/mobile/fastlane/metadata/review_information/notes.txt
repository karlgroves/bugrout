BugRout is an offline-first map and routing app for evacuations. Map coverage is Maryland only for now; other states will follow.

TESTING OUTSIDE MARYLAND
Because the only published map is Maryland, please turn on the demo location, which places you in downtown Baltimore and drives along each route once you start it:

1. On first launch, read and accept the disclaimer. Allowing location is optional when using the demo location.
2. Open Settings > Offline Maps and download Maryland (about 140 MB; Wi-Fi recommended).
3. In Settings, turn on "Demo location". An amber DEMO LOCATION badge then shows on the map and during navigation. It switches off when the app restarts.
4. On the Map tab, tap BUG OUT, search for "Annapolis", and choose a result.
5. Review the route preview (distance, time, warnings), then tap Go.
6. Turn-by-turn navigation starts, and the demo position drives the route at about 30 mph, so turns and voice prompts arrive as they would in a car. Tap Stop to end the trip at any time.

Routing needs a data connection; the downloaded map works without one.

BACKGROUND LOCATION (Guideline 2.5.4)
BugRout asks for "Always" location and declares the location background mode only so that turn-by-turn guidance and off-route warnings continue while the phone is locked or another app is in front during an evacuation, as the purpose strings say: "BugRout uses your location continuously during navigation to provide turn-by-turn directions and deviation alerts, even when the app is in the background." Location is not used in the background at any other time.

EMERGENCY TEXT MESSAGE
During navigation, the red phone button beside Stop ("Send location to emergency contacts") opens the standard iOS message composer, addressed to the user's own emergency contacts (Settings > Emergency Contacts) and pre-filled with their position, destination and arrival time. Nothing is sent unless the user taps Send in the composer.

SAFETY
Route guidance is advisory only. The app tells users to follow official evacuation orders, and an ADVISORY ONLY badge is shown throughout navigation.
