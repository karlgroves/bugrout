BugRout helps you plan and drive an evacuation route. Always follow official evacuation orders and instructions first: BugRout is an advisory tool, not a replacement for them.

WHAT IT DOES

• Offline Maps — Download your state's map before you need it. Once downloaded, the map works with no cell service. Maryland is available today; more states are coming.

• Threat-Aware Routing — Severe-weather alerts from the National Weather Service are shown on the map, and route calculation steers around them. Routes are calculated by BugRout's routing service, so planning a route needs a data connection.

• Three Taps to Navigation — Launch, tap Bug Out, choose a destination, then Go. Save up to 3 evacuation plans with a destination and optional water stops, so a plan is one tap away.

• Turn-by-Turn Guidance — Voice directions, a clear next-turn card, and an "Advisory only" reminder for the whole trip.

• Water Sources — See nearby water sources from USGS and OpenStreetMap on the map.

• Emergency Contacts — Save up to 5 contacts. One tap opens a text message to them with your current location, destination and ETA; nothing is sent until you tap Send.

• Privacy — No account and no advertising. What leaves your device is listed in the in-app privacy policy: address searches go to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, and the start and end of a route go to BugRout's routing service.

DATA SOURCES
Maps from OpenStreetMap. Weather alerts from the National Weather Service. Water sources from USGS and OpenStreetMap.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
BugRout provides advisory routing only. Routes are based on map data and published weather alerts and may not reflect real-time road conditions. Do not rely solely on this app for life-safety decisions. Always follow official evacuation orders.
